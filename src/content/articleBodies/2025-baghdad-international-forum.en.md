From February 21 to 23, 2025, the 7th Baghdad International Forum—"Exploring Regional Connectivity" (7th Baghdad Dialogue: Regional Connectivity and Development)—was held in Iraq. Jodie Wen, postdoctoral fellow and assistant research fellow at the Center for International Security and Strategy (CISS), Tsinghua University, and Director of International Communication for the China Forum, was invited to attend as a representative of Chinese scholars and took part as a panel speaker in the session on "Regional Economic and Energy Cooperation."

The Baghdad International Forum is jointly hosted by the Iraqi Institute for Dialogue and Germany's Center for Applied Research in Partnership with the Orient (CARPO), and has long received the support of the Iraqi government. Iraqi Prime Minister Mohammed Shia al-Sudani, President Abdul Latif Rashid, Speaker of Parliament Mahmoud al-Mashhadani, and Bafel Talabani, President of the Patriotic Union of Kurdistan, attended the forum and invited the participating guests to join closed-door discussions. The meeting also invited nearly 40 political figures and leading think-tank scholars from the United States, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and other regions.

Iraq is currently a focal point of geopolitical contestation in the Middle East and, at the same time, an important country for the Belt and Road Initiative that China is building together with other countries. With its theme of "Exploring Regional Connectivity," the meeting was an important platform for Chinese scholars to engage with the outside world and exchange views.

While in Iraq, Wen also visited the Chinese Embassy in Iraq and exchanged views with Political Counselor Xu Haifeng and others on the current situation in Iraq and the Middle East.
