On April 28, 2026, the China Forum of the Center for International Security and Strategy (CISS) at Tsinghua University successfully held the 10th "China Forum Expert-Media Dialogue." The theme of this session was "The US–Israel–Iran Conflict: Global Impact and Pathways to Break the Deadlock." The participating experts held in-depth discussions on the trajectory and impact of the US–Israel–Iran conflict, the root causes of the behavior of the parties to the conflict, and China's role and contribution.

The experts noted that the United States, together with Israel, launched military strikes against Iran, triggering turmoil in the region and across the globe. This act damaged the United States' global image and had a negative impact on its alliance relationships with countries in Europe, East Asia and the Gulf. In the face of the tense situation in the Middle East, China has consistently pushed for a peaceful settlement of the conflict, worked to maintain security and stability in the Middle East, and sought to play a constructive role in resolving regional conflicts.

Duan Jiuzhou, assistant research fellow at the Institute for International and Area Studies at Tsinghua University; Gong Jiong, special expert of the China Forum and professor at the School of Economics, University of International Business and Economics; and Jesse Marks, PhD researcher at the Centre for Arab and Islamic Studies at the Australian National University, delivered keynote remarks. Jodie Wen, CISS postdoctoral fellow and assistant research fellow and Director of International Communication for the China Forum, moderated the expert remarks and the media discussion.

Media representatives attending the event came from Bloomberg News, Brazil's O Globo, TASS, the Mainichi Shimbun, Süddeutsche Zeitung, The Asahi Shimbun, Nikkei, NHK, The Yomiuri Shimbun, China-US Focus, Lianhe Zaobao, the South China Morning Post, China Daily, CGTN, China.org.cn and others.
