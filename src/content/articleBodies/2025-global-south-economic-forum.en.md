On June 14–18, 2025, the first Global South Economic Forum (GSEF2025) was held in Abu Dhabi, the capital of the United Arab Emirates. Jodie Wen, a postdoctoral fellow and assistant research fellow at the Center for International Security and Strategy (CISS), Tsinghua University, and Director of International Communication for the China Forum, attended as a representative of Chinese scholars at the invitation of the organizers and moderated the sub-forum "Decolonizing Media Narratives: Empowering Voices and Perspectives."

The Global South Economic Forum (GSEF) is co-hosted by the Global South Geoeconomics Center, the Anwar Gargash Diplomatic Academy of the UAE and the Academy of Contemporary China and World Studies (ACCWS) of the China International Communications Group, with the Emirates Center for Strategic Studies and Research (ECSSR), the Center for International Security and Strategy of Tsinghua University and the School of Journalism and Communication of Tsinghua University participating as co-organizers. The forum aims to explore the evolving role of the Global South in a rapidly changing global geoeconomic and geostrategic landscape. By bringing together experts and key stakeholders and focusing on strategic cooperation, the forum is committed to building a fairer and more effective global economic framework that reflects the vision and potential of Global South countries.

During her stay in the UAE, Wen and the China International Communications Group delegation visited the Dubai Public Policy Research Center and the Emirates Center for Strategic Studies and Research, and also called at the Chinese Embassy in the UAE, where they exchanged views with Minister-Counselor Zhao Liang, Rao Dajun, director of the Political and Press Affairs Office, and others on the current situation in the UAE and the Middle East.
