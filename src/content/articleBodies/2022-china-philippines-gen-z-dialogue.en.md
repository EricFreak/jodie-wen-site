On December 4, 2022, Jodie Wen, assistant research fellow at the Center for International Security and Strategy (CISS), Tsinghua University, was invited to attend the China–Philippines Gen-Z Cross-Cultural Online Dialogue, hosted jointly by the Beijing People's Association for Friendship with Foreign Countries and the Association for Philippines-China Understanding.

Guests at the event, including Chinese Ambassador to the Philippines Huang Xilian, Liu Yuhong, Vice President of the Beijing People's Association for Friendship with Foreign Countries, and Lambino, President of the Association for Philippines-China Understanding, sent their congratulations on the event through congratulatory letters and remarks, and encouraged young people from both countries to make greater contributions to enhancing people-to-people friendship between China and the Philippines through exchange activities.

Wen delivered a keynote speech at the first session of the forum, “Opportunities and Challenges in China–Philippines Cross-Cultural Exchange.” Reviewing the history of exchanges between the two countries in the political, diplomatic, economic and trade, and cultural fields, she noted that the long-term stable and friendly relations between China and the Philippines in the political and diplomatic fields are the foundation of cross-cultural exchange between the two sides. In Wen's view, the current world order is undergoing drastic change, and the overlapping effects of the Ukraine crisis and the COVID-19 pandemic have reached the Asia-Pacific region as well; China and the Philippines should manage risks and properly handle disputes in order to meet the challenges their relations may face. Wen put forward suggestions on China–Philippines cross-cultural exchange in three respects: first, uphold the principles of mutual respect, openness and inclusiveness, and mutual learning; second, give full play to the bridging and bonding role of the media, especially the mass media; third, engage young people and pool the strength of “Gen Z.”
