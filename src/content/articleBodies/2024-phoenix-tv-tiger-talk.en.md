Jodie Wen, a postdoctoral fellow and assistant research fellow at the Center for International Security and Strategy (CISS), Tsinghua University, and director of international communications at the China Forum, was recently invited to record an episode of Phoenix TV's current affairs program Tiger Talk, joining an in-depth discussion on the theme "One Year of the Israel–Hamas War: Will the Middle East Conflict Spiral Out of Control?"

The recording came at the one-year mark of the current round of the Israel–Hamas conflict. With the conflict between Israel and Iran escalating, the situation in the Middle East had become extremely tense, regional security conditions had continued to deteriorate, and global concern over where the situation in the Middle East is heading had deepened. The interview brought together a range of viewpoints: Wen and the other guests discussed the course of the Israel–Hamas conflict, the escalation of the confrontation between Iran and Israel, and the overall situation in the Middle East, and shared insights from their own research.

The interview kept close to international hot topics and offered incisive views. Wen not only used her professional expertise to give viewers a strategic perspective for understanding the Middle East situation, but also drew on her fieldwork experience visiting Middle Eastern countries to offer first-hand observation and analysis. In addition, as a scholar specializing in US and Middle East studies, Wen particularly emphasized the key role the United States plays in the Middle East situation. She noted that, as the US election approached, the Middle East situation could become a variable affecting US domestic politics, and that the election result and the trajectory of US–Israel relations would also have an important impact on the future Middle East situation.

The episode will air on Phoenix TV on the evening of October 19 (Saturday), Beijing time. Tiger Talk is a talk show on Phoenix TV hosted by Hu Yihu. Each week it selects major events and focal or hot topics from social, cultural and other fields, and invites the people involved as well as scholars, experts and public figures from various sectors as guests; each week it distills hot social and cultural topics, with the host, guests and audience each holding different views and ideas and speaking frankly.
