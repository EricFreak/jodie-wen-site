On September 12, Jodie Wen—postdoctoral fellow and assistant research fellow at the Center for International Security and Strategy (CISS), Tsinghua University, and Director of International Communication for the China Forum—spoke at the invitation of the China Association of Enterprises with Foreign Investment (CAEFI), sharing her latest research findings with the company representatives in attendance under the theme "The Trajectory of the U.S. Election and Predictions for U.S. Policy toward China." Wen gave a detailed account of where the U.S. election currently stands, analyzed the possible course of this year's election, and explored in depth the differences between the two parties' China policies and their implications.

Drawing on the governing philosophies and policy positions of the two candidates, she focused on their potential impact on China's economic development and foreign investment, among other areas, and put forward recommendations for responding. The meeting not only offered the participating companies valuable forward-looking insights and helped them better understand U.S. political trends and U.S. policy toward China, but also helped them seize future development opportunities in a complex international environment, strengthen economic cooperation between China and other countries, and optimize their investment strategies in China. The event was moderated by Peng Ye, Secretary-General of the Legal Committee of CAEFI and Director of Government Affairs at Procter & Gamble, and Ye Nan, Director of Novartis China.

It drew representatives of more than 30 Fortune Global 500 foreign-invested enterprises, including Procter & Gamble, Novartis, Danfoss, Johnson & Johnson, Sonova, Starbucks and Shiseido. Founded in Beijing in November 1987, CAEFI is a nationwide, non-profit social organization under the administration of the Ministry of Commerce and registered with the Ministry of Civil Affairs, composed mainly of foreign-invested enterprises operating in China together with enterprises invested in by compatriots from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan and by overseas Chinese.

In line with the state's policies, laws and regulations on opening up and encouraging foreign investment, the association works to serve its members and investors, guides members to operate in compliance with the law, and safeguards members' lawful rights and interests; it also seeks to enhance mutual understanding, friendship and cooperation among members and between members and the government, and to encourage members to play an active role in China's socialist economic development and in international economic cooperation.
